Why do I only get rejections? – First self-help tips

  1. 1

    Stop the self-doubt – you are not the problem.

    Rejections don’t mean you are “not good enough” – often they mean:

    • Your documents don’t properly reflect your skills
    • You objectively fit, but your profile doesn’t show it clearly enough
    • You use job portals that don’t suit you at all
    • Your CV sounds like many others – but not like you

    → Question for you: which of these could you improve?

  2. 2

    Reflect on your application strategy

    Ask yourself the following questions very honestly or use an AI such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Take a screenshot of your CV without personal data and upload it to the AI.

    • Am I applying for suitable jobs – or for whatever “might somehow fit”?
    • Do my cover letter and CV speak the company’s language?

  4. 3

    Checklist for your application:

    Ask the AI:

    • Is your CV clear, structured and visually appealing?
    • Can I see within 10 seconds what you can do and what you are looking for?
    • Do you show motivation, not just qualifications?
  5. 4

    Use the power of AI for fine-tuning

    Prompt for ChatGPT:

    Prompt 1:

    “Optimise my CV for the position of [job title] – here is my text...”

    Prompt 2:

    “Write a convincing short profile for an application in [e.g. customer service / administration / sales]”
