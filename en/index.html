Frequently asked questions

Clear answers

For companies

What does recruiting cost with you? +
Initial placements range from approx. €850 to €5,000 depending on effort. Follow-up placements are considerably cheaper. Billing is exclusively on a fee basis according to actual time spent.
How long does a placement take? +
Depending on urgency. We start with free job portals and active sourcing, then gradually add paid channels if needed.
Do I keep all data and access? +
Yes. You receive all applications, access details, job accounts including passwords as well as all created materials. You remain completely independent.
Is there government funding? +
From autumn, up to 80% government funding is possible. Feel free to ask me about it directly.

For job seekers

What does job coaching cost? +
The coaching usually comprises 4 to 40 teaching units, depending on goal and need. Some clients have developed a clear career perspective from as little as approx. €260. With an AVGS voucher, the coaching is 100% free for eligible participants.

Concerned about payment?
We'll find a solution together! Just talk to me!
What is an AVGS voucher? +
The Activation and Placement Voucher (AVGS) is issued by the German Employment Agency (Agentur für Arbeit) and allows people registered as job-seeking to use coaching and job placement with certified providers free of charge.
How does coaching work? +
We start with a free introductory call. Then we analyse where you stand and which of the 10 modules will help you most. Everything online – conveniently from home.
Can I end the coaching at any time? +
Yes, you control the process individually. If you no longer need support, the coaching can be adjusted or ended at any time.

No applications to your job ad?

If no one or hardly anyone applies, it's often not just down to the labour market. There are frequently several reasons:

  • The job ad doesn't address the target group clearly enough.
  • The job title isn't found properly on Google or job boards.
  • The benefits aren't presented clearly or attractively enough.
  • The application process is too complicated.
  • The ad is visible on the wrong channels.
  • Applicant communication takes too long.
  • The position doesn't stand out enough from other offers.

Together we examine where applicants drop off and how your recruiting can be improved.

Frequently asked questions about recruiting

Why can't I find suitable employees?This can be due to the job ad, the job title, visibility, channels, wording or the application process. I review with you where improvements are possible.
What can I do if no one applies to my job ad?Then it's worth taking a close look at your ad. Clearer wording, better search terms, understandable benefits, a simple application process and the right platforms often help.
Who helps me with recruiting?I support small and medium-sized businesses – from the job ad to approaching applicants to pre-selecting suitable candidates.
Can I outsource my recruiting?Yes. You can outsource individual recruiting tasks or the entire process: writing the job ad, pre-screening applications, contacting applicants or actively searching for suitable candidates.

Is an external recruiter worthwhile for small businesses?Yes, especially if you don't have your own HR department or no time in everyday business for active candidate sourcing – without having to create an in-house HR position.
How do I get more suitable applications?Through a clear job ad, good visibility, the right search terms, active outreach and a simple application process.
Who can improve my job ad?I review your job ad, make the wording clearer and tailor it more closely to the right target group.
How do I find skilled workers in my region?Through the right job portals, Google visibility, networks, social media, active sourcing and a clear employer message.
What does recruiting support cost?The cost depends on the scope. You can start small (e.g. a job ad analysis) or book more support.

AVGS coaching with a voucher from the Employment Agency or the Jobcenter

If you are job-seeking, unemployed or threatened by unemployment, you can apply for an AVGS voucher at the Employment Agency (Agentur für Arbeit) or the Jobcenter. AVGS stands for Activation and Placement Voucher.

With an AVGS voucher, job coaching or application coaching is usually 100% free for eligible participants. The costs are then covered by the voucher.

I'm happy to help you formulate your goal for the coaching clearly so that you can speak to your contact person at the Employment Agency or the Jobcenter well prepared.
